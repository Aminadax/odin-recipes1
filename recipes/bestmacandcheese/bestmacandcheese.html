The Best Baked Mac and Cheese

Description

This is the ultimate comforting baked mac and cheese. It features a rich, velvety homemade cheese sauce made with sharp cheddar and Gruyère, perfectly coated macaroni, and a golden, crispy breadcrumb topping.

It is baked to bubbly perfection, making it the star side dish for any holiday or family dinner!

Ingredients

  • 16 oz elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups whole milk (warmed)
  • 1 cup heavy cream (warmed)
  • 3 cups sharp cheddar cheese, freshly shredded
  • 2 cups Gruyère cheese, freshly shredded
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup Panko breadcrumbs (optional, for topping)

Steps

  1. Boil the elbow macaroni in salted water until just slightly under al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat, then whisk in the flour to create a roux. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Slowly pour in the warmed milk and heavy cream while whisking constantly. Simmer until the sauce thickens.
  4. Turn off the heat and stir in the garlic powder, salt, pepper, and 4 cups of the shredded cheese until completely melted and smooth.
  5. Toss the cooked macaroni into the cheese sauce until evenly coated.
  6. Pour half the pasta into a greased baking dish, sprinkle a layer of the remaining cheese, add the rest of the pasta, and top with the last bit of cheese and Panko breadcrumbs.
  7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 to 25 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and the top is golden brown.
